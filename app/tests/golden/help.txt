usage: produce.py [-h] [--rate RATE] [--duration DURATION] [--n-users N_USERS]
                  [--volatility-per-sec VOLATILITY_PER_SEC]
                  [--mean-base-amount MEAN_BASE_AMOUNT] [--late-pct LATE_PCT]
                  [--duplicate-pct DUPLICATE_PCT]
                  [--price-spike-per-min PRICE_SPIKE_PER_MIN]
                  [--fat-finger-per-min FAT_FINGER_PER_MIN]
                  [--volume-burst-per-min VOLUME_BURST_PER_MIN]
                  [--account-burst-per-min ACCOUNT_BURST_PER_MIN]
                  [--seed SEED] [--dry-run]

F1 synthetic FX trade producer (Phase 1)

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --rate RATE           trades per second (default: 500.0)
  --duration DURATION   seconds to run; 0 = until Ctrl+C (default: 60.0)
  --n-users N_USERS     distinct users (default: 1000)
  --volatility-per-sec VOLATILITY_PER_SEC
                        random-walk step of the mid per second (default:
                        5e-05)
  --mean-base-amount MEAN_BASE_AMOUNT
                        average trade size in base currency (default: 5000.0)
  --late-pct LATE_PCT   share of trades with an old event time (default: 0.02)
  --duplicate-pct DUPLICATE_PCT
                        share of trades sent twice (default: 0.005)
  --price-spike-per-min PRICE_SPIKE_PER_MIN
                        injected price spikes per minute (default: 2.0)
  --fat-finger-per-min FAT_FINGER_PER_MIN
                        injected oversized trades per minute (default: 1.0)
  --volume-burst-per-min VOLUME_BURST_PER_MIN
                        injected pair volume bursts per minute (default: 0.5)
  --account-burst-per-min ACCOUNT_BURST_PER_MIN
                        injected single-user bursts per minute (default: 0.5)
  --seed SEED           random seed; same seed = same trades (default: None)
  --dry-run             don't connect to Kafka; print samples and Avro sizes
